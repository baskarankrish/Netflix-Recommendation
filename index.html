Netflix Recommendation App

Which shows get binge-watched together, and a recommendation app that turns that into “Because you watched…”.

Python · Vanilla JS · Correlation · Clustering · WCAG AA

I analysed 18,000 viewing records from 2,000 subscribers (about 708,000 hours watched across 9 shows) to answer a practical question: if someone loves one show, which other shows should we recommend next?

Pick Dark and the app recommends Umbrella Academy (96% match) and Stranger Things (84%). Big Bang Theory is the most-watched show, yet it predicts nothing: picking it alone gives every other show a 0% match.
